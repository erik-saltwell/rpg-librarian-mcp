# Extract from Brendan

Find RPG content in Brendan's OneDrive that is missing from my library, copy it into my inbox while preserving his folder structure, and process it through a large initial import followed by nightly runs with email notifications when review is needed.

## Quality rubric

This approved rubric judges the quality of the process for discovering and importing missing content from Brendan's collection into my library.

- **Discovery accuracy:** Correctly distinguishes content missing from my library from content I already own, despite differences in filenames or organization.
- **Organization preservation:** Retains Brendan's folder structure and the useful context his organization provides as content enters my inbox.
- **Operational reliability:** Makes dependable progress through the large initial import and nightly runs, including recovery from interruptions without losing or repeating work.
- **Resource efficiency:** Uses reasonable time, bandwidth, and storage for both the initial backlog and ongoing discovery.
- **Unattended operation:** Supports frequent runs that make useful progress without requiring my review or intervention. Values allowing imports to proceed when matching is uncertain rather than making review a prerequisite for copying.
- **Review clarity and effort:** Keeps my involvement manageable, with emails that explain what needs attention and enough context to make decisions.

Reliability and efficiency are distinct: a process could recover correctly from interruptions but still waste bandwidth rescanning or downloading content.

Unattended operation concerns how much progress the process can make without me; review clarity and effort concerns the usefulness and burden of reviews that do occur.

Rubric revision: Added unattended operation at the user's request during workshop. The user prefers frequent runs without review blocking progress and rejected holding uncertain matches for review before copying. Discovery accuracy remains valuable, but uncertainty alone should not make copying depend on review.
